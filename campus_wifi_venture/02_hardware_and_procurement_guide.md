### Why This Specific Hardware?
1. **Level 4 RouterOS License (Included Free):** Allows up to 200 concurrent active hotspot sessions. No recurring license fees to MikroTik.
2. **Built-in 2.4 GHz 802.11b/g/n Access Point:** Broadcasts directly across rooms and walls without needing an external antenna or access point.
3. **5V Micro-USB Power Input:** Can be powered by a standard 2A smartphone charger or a portable power bank during hostel power outages.
4. **Bandwidth Queueing Engine:** Gives each connected student guaranteed speed limits (e.g., 3 Mbps down / 1 Mbps up) so no single user freezes the network.
