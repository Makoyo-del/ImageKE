### EQUIPMENT FINANCING & REVENUE-SHARE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on this _____ day of _______________, 2026, by and between:
1. **OPERATOR & SOLE OWNER**: **Duncan Makoyo**, National ID No. _______________, residing in Kisii, Kenya (hereinafter referred to as the **"Operator"**).
2. **FINANCIAL INVESTOR**: ________________________________, National ID No. _______________, residing in Kisii, Kenya (hereinafter referred to as the **"Investor"**).

#### 1. Nature of the Agreement
The Investor agrees to provide capital to finance hardware equipment for a commercial campus Wi-Fi network managed solely by the Operator. This agreement constitutes a **finite debt financing agreement with a revenue-based buyout**, and explicitly **does NOT** constitute a general partnership, equity partnership, joint venture, or corporation.

#### 2. Capital Contribution & Fixed Return Cap
* **Principal Invested:** The Investor contributes a one-time, non-refundable principal sum of **KES _______________** (e.g., KES 3,000).
* **Agreed Total Return Cap:** In consideration of the investment, the Operator agrees to pay the Investor a total cumulative return of **KES _______________** (e.g., KES 4,500), representing full return of principal plus an agreed return on investment.

#### 3. Repayment Schedule from Net Profits
* Repayments shall be disbursed monthly, strictly derived from **Net Profit** (defined as: Gross Subscriber Revenue minus ISP Connectivity Costs minus Essential Hosting/Transaction Fees).
* The Investor shall receive **thirty percent (30%) of the Net Monthly Profit** until the cumulative payments reach the Agreed Total Return Cap.
* The Operator reserves the right to accelerate repayment and settle the remaining balance at any time.

#### 4. Automatic Termination & Extinguishment of Rights
* The moment the cumulative payments made to the Investor reach the Agreed Total Return Cap, **this Agreement terminates automatically with immediate effect**.
* Following termination, the Investor holds **zero ongoing financial interest, zero profit entitlement, and zero claims** against the Operator or the network.

#### 5. Sole Ownership of Intellectual Property & Physical Assets
* **100% Asset Ownership:** All physical routers, modems, switches, cables, and enclosures purchased or utilized in this project remain the sole, exclusive property of Duncan Makoyo.
* **100% Intellectual Property:** All software, captive portal designs, backend APIs, databases, domain names, and brand trademarks are the sole intellectual property of Duncan Makoyo. The Investor acquires zero rights, licenses, or claims to any technology or code.

#### 6. Operational Non-Interference
The Operator retains sole and absolute authority over technical architecture, network configurations, pricing tiers, operational policies, and maintenance schedules. The Investor shall have no management or operational authority.

### INFRASTRUCTURE HOSTING & REVENUE-SHARE JOINT VENTURE AGREEMENT

**Between:**
1. **Duncan Makoyo** (Makoyocart Ventures, Reg: `BN-WLSP9KP9`) — Technology Operator & Sole Owner
2. **Sam Chonjo** — Infrastructure & Capital Partner

#### Summary of Binding Terms:
1. **Asset Segregation**: Duncan Makoyo owns 100% of MikroTik router, software, captive portal, backend API, database, merchant accounts, and IP. Sam Chonjo owns 100% of the 5G modem gateway.
2. **The 5-Tier Waterfall**:
   * Tier 1: Gateway fees (~1.5% Paystack/M-Pesa)
   * Tier 2: **3.0% Statutory KRA Tax Reserve** (withheld at source for Duncan's KRA compliance)
   * Tier 3: **100% Line Cost Recovery** (First KES 3,000/= remitted directly to Sam Chonjo)
   * Tier 4: **60% Net Distributable Profit** to Duncan Makoyo
   * Tier 5: **40% Net Distributable Profit** to Sam Chonjo
3. **Shortfall Protection**: If revenue < KES 3,000, all net revenue goes to the ISP line; neither draws profit.
4. **Indemnity**: Sam Chonjo explicitly indemnifies Duncan Makoyo against tax liabilities from Sam's share.
5. **Non-Circumvention**: 6-month non-compete within 100m; Sam cannot duplicate or share router scripts or portal code.
6. **Exit**: 14 days written notice; Duncan retrieves MikroTik; Sam retains 5G modem.
